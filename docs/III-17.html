Book III · Proposition 17

III.17

From a given point to draw a straight line touching a given circle.Heath, 1908

Every step, checked

  • the circle has positive radiusgiven
  • the point is outsidegiven
  • the angle at the point of contact is rightby III.31
  • so PT touches the circle and does not cut itby III.16

What it needs, and what needs it

Needs: I.10 III.16 III.31

Rests on: C.N.1, C.N.3, C.N.4, C.N.5, Def.10, Def.15, Def.17, Def.4, Post.5

Depth: 14 steps of argument above the first principles. Parallel postulate: needed.

What it takes on trust

  • a circle-circle intersection is used, though no postulate asserts that two circles meet (×4)continuity
  • a line-circle intersection is used, though no postulate asserts that a line meets a circle (×2)continuity

The proposition as code

@proposition(
    "III.17",
    CONSTRUCTION,
    sample=lambda rng: samples.points_round_a_circle(rng, 1)
    + (1 + Fraction(rng.randint(2, 8), 4),),
)
def prop_III_17(o: Point, a: Point, beyond) -> Out:
    """From a point outside the circle, draw a line touching it."""
    hypothesis("the circle has positive radius", o != a)
    hypothesis("the point is outside", sign(beyond - 1) > 0)
    around = circle(o, a, "the given circle")
    outside = posit(Point(o.x + beyond * (a.x - o.x), o.y + beyond * (a.y - o.y)), "P")

    # The point of contact lies on the circle on OP as diameter (III.31), which
    # is how the right angle at the contact gets built rather than assumed.
    middle = posit(prop_I_10(o, outside).midpoint, "M")
    helper = circle_with_radius2(middle, len2(middle, o), "the circle on OP")
    touch = posit(meet(helper, around)[0], "T")
    tangent = line(outside, touch, "the tangent PT")
    line(o, touch, "the radius to the point of contact")

    claim("the angle at the point of contact is right", "III.31",
          right_angle(o, touch, outside))
    claim("so PT touches the circle and does not cut it", "III.16",
          on_circle(touch, around)
          and not any(inside_circle(Point(touch.x + Fraction(k, 4) * (outside.x - touch.x),
                                          touch.y + Fraction(k, 4) * (outside.y - touch.y)),
                                    around) for k in range(1, 5)))
    return Out(tangent=tangent, contact=touch)
